The 2–4–6 Game

A short exercise in how we test our ideas.

The following sequence of three numbers is consistent with a simple mathematical rule:

2, 4, 6

Order matters. Your job is to figure out the rule.

Before you test anything, write down what you currently think the rule is.

Next, you'll test sets of numbers of your own. After each test you'll restate the rule and say how confident you are (0–100%). The game ends, and you'll be told whether you were right or wrong, when you say you are 90% or more certain you've guessed the rule, or after 10 rounds.

The rule

The three numbers must be in increasing order.
That's it. Any three integers where the first is less than the second and the second is less than the third — for example (−10, 3, 43534534) or (3, 4, 5).
